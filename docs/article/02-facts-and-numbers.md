## KV cache dtype experiment — `reports/longctx/longctx-fp8kv-experiment.json`

fp8 KV cache (FP8 weights): needle found in 6/12 attempts across targets [16000, 22000, 26000, 30000] tokens (3 seeds each). Every success had a prompt of at most 20.9k tokens; every failure had at least 24.5k tokens, and every failed answer began with `duct Register Register R`. bf16 KV cache on the same series (`longctx-kv-auto.json`, NVFP4 weights): 12/12. Control with FP8 weights and the bf16 KV cache (final suite, same prompt design, 3 attempts per size): ~2k 3/3, ~8k 3/3, ~15k 3/3, ~23k 3/3, ~28k 3/3. The boundary lies between ~21k and ~24.5k tokens for this model, prompt design and vLLM version; the earlier interactive series (not saved) suggested it is fuzzy around 22-24k.
